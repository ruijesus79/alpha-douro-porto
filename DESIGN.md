# DESIGN.md — Alpha Douro Quiet-Luxury Brand System

## Visual Philosophy (Vignelli × Rams × Riva)
- **Quiet Editorial Restraint:** Clarity in logistics (prices, 2h duration, Pontão A, max 10 guests), poetic restraint on the onboard experience. Never shout; never clutter.
- **Zero Cliché Rule:** No stock tourists, no wine brand labels, no Polaroid references, no Pastéis de Nata/cheese/chocolate promises on standard tours.

## Color Tokens
- **Atlantic Midnight Navy (Primary Dark):** `#0A1624` (deep canvas) / `#06111D` (abyss)
- **Warm Cotton Ivory (Primary Light):** `#F6F2E8` / `#F6F3EC`
- **Brushed Champagne Gold (Accent Foil):** `#E6CB98` (on dark) / `#8A6B33` & `#C9A86A` (on light)
- **Terracotta Wax Seal (Editorial Stamp):** `#A44A2B`
- **Atlantic Slate (Secondary Ink):** `#5C6F84` / `#9CB2C8`

## Typography System
- **Display Serif:** `Cormorant Garamond` (500–700, italic highlights for poetic lines)
- **UI & Body Sans:** `Plus Jakarta Sans` (400–600, clean European clarity)
- **Nautical Telemetry & Coordinates:** `JetBrains Mono` (400–600, uppercase wide tracking `0.14em–0.24em`, `tabular-nums`)

## Surface & Social Grid System
- **Instagram Grid (`@alphadouro`):** 100% pure-graphic bilingual carousels (`1080×1350`, Slide 1 PT `PT | EN →`, Slide 2 EN `← PT | EN`), alternating Midnight Navy and Warm Cotton Ivory in a strict 3:4 checkerboard rhythm.
- **Highlight Medallions:** `1080×1080` Midnight Navy radial glow with dual fine Champagne Gold hairline rings and custom geometric SVG emblems (`I–V`).
- **Mobile Web Performance:** Native 120fps touch scroll (no scroll hijacking on touch viewports), `0px` horizontal overflow (`375px` & `390px`), minimum `44px` tap targets.
